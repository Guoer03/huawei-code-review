# JavaScript rule profile

Source: `openharmony-javascript` (OpenHarmony public guide).

- `OH-JS-DECL-001`: use `const` or `let`, not `var`.
- `OH-JS-SECURITY-001`: do not use `eval`, `with`, or dynamically constructed functions.
- `OH-JS-FORMAT-001`: use two spaces for indentation and do not use tab characters.
- `OH-JS-FORMAT-002`: keep lines within 120 characters except documented URLs or generated diagnostics.
- `OH-JS-CONTROL-001`: brace conditional and loop bodies, even for one statement.
- `OH-JS-NAMING-001`: use UpperCamelCase for classes/enums/namespaces and lowerCamelCase for variables, methods, and parameters.
- `OH-JS-EQUALITY-001`: prefer `===` and `!==` over loose equality.
